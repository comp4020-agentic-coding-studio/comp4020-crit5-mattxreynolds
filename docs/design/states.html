Direction A, isometric — the four states

One flat grid engine. Height is drawn, never simulated — a wall is still just “stops the ball”.

1 · Level 1, idle

Ball, hole two tiles away, one card. Tile seams make the “2” countable. No arrows yet — nothing is offered until a card is held.

2 · Card held

Tapping the card raises it and reveals the directions on the three reachable tiles. One of them holes out; the other two lose the level.

3 · Mid-play, level 3

Wall as a raised block, sand stippled, slope as an actual tilted plane. Spent card greyed. Restart is always available in the top bar.
